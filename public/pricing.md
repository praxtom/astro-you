## Credit packs (one-time purchase)

- 50 credits: ₹49 (₹0.98/credit) — For a quick first consult or one small report.
- 120 credits: ₹99 (₹0.82/credit) — Enough for a focused astrologer sitting.
- 300 credits: ₹249 (₹0.83/credit) — For deeper guidance, reports, and follow-up questions.
- 700 credits: ₹499 (₹0.71/credit) — Best value for regular weekly guidance.

## What credits are spent on

- Astrologer sitting: from 5 credits per minute
- AI Jyotish chat: draws from the same balance
- PDF natal report: draws from the same balance

Purchased credits remain in the wallet. Subscription credits refresh monthly.
